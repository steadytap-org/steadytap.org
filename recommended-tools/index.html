Easier phones

Often the easiest phone is the one you already have, set up more simply. Try the free options first.

Free

iPhone with Assistive Access (free setting)

Apple’s simpler layout with larger buttons and only the apps you choose. Built into recent iPhones; a family member can set it up.

Good to know: Needs iOS 17 or later.

Official page for iPhone with Assistive Access (free setting)

Free

Samsung Galaxy Easy mode (free setting)

A simpler home screen with larger icons and text on many Samsung phones.

Good to know: Menu names vary by model.

Official page for Samsung Galaxy Easy mode (free setting)

Lively Jitterbug Smart4 phone

A smartphone designed for older adults, with a simple list-style menu.

Good to know: Requires a Lively service plan. Compare plan prices before buying.

Official page for Lively Jitterbug Smart4 phone

Lively Jitterbug Flip2 phone

A flip phone with large buttons for people who mostly call.

Good to know: Requires a Lively service plan. Few apps.

Official page for Lively Jitterbug Flip2 phone

Big-button TV remotes

A simple remote can replace a confusing one. Check that it lists your TV brand before you buy.

Flipper big-button universal remote

A small remote with a few large buttons (power, channel, volume) that a helper sets up once.

Good to know: Check the maker’s compatibility list for your TV and cable box.

Official page for Flipper big-button universal remote

Big-button remotes in general

Look for: under 10 buttons, raised labels, and a cover or lock for setup buttons.

Good to know: Reviews from people with your TV brand are the best guide.

Call blockers

Start with the free tools from your phone and phone company. See our guide “Block spam calls and texts.”

Free

Your phone’s built-in blocking (free)

iPhone can silence unknown callers; many Android phones can filter spam calls.

Good to know: Calls from new numbers (like a doctor’s office) may go to voicemail.

Read our free guide for Your phone’s built-in blocking (free)

Free

Your phone company’s free app (free)

AT&T ActiveArmor, T-Mobile Scam Shield, and Verizon Call Filter each have a free version.

Good to know: Paid upgrades are optional.

Official page for Your phone company’s free app (free)

Home phone (landline) call blocker

A small box between your phone and the wall that blocks numbers you choose. Some cordless phones have call blocking built in.

Good to know: Check that it works with your phone service (landline or internet phone).

Password managers

A password manager remembers your passwords so you don’t have to. See our guide “Use a password manager.”

Free

Apple Passwords app (free)

Built into iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Works with your Apple Account.

Good to know: Needs iOS 18 or later. Best if you mostly use Apple devices.

Official page for Apple Passwords app (free)

Free

Google Password Manager (free)

Built into Android phones and the Chrome browser.

Good to know: Best if you mostly use Android or Chrome.

Official page for Google Password Manager (free)

Bitwarden

Has a free plan and works on iPhone, Android, and computers.

Good to know: Paid family plan available. Set up with a helper the first time.

Official page for Bitwarden

1Password

A paid manager with a family plan, so a trusted family member can help if you get locked out.

Good to know: Subscription. A free trial is usually offered.

Official page for 1Password
