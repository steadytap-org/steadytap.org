How to Set Reminders With Your Voice

What this helps with: Asking your phone or smart speaker to remind you of things, like a phone call or an appointment.

With Siri (iPhone)

  1. Wake up Siri. Say “Siri” or “Hey Siri,” or press and hold the side button (or the Home button on older iPhones).
    • What you’ll see: A glowing light around the edge of the screen, or a colorful ball at the bottom.
  2. Say your reminder. For example:
    • “Remind me tomorrow at 3 p.m. to call Tara.”
    • “Remind me to water the plants every Monday at 9 a.m.”
  3. Check it. Siri shows the reminder on screen. It’s saved in the Reminders app.
    • What you’ll tap: Nothing, unless you want to change it. Then open the Reminders app and tap the reminder.

With Google (Android)

On most Android phones, Gemini is now the voice helper. It replaced Google Assistant on phones starting in September 2026.

  1. Wake up Gemini. Say “Hey Google,” or press and hold the power button. On some phones, you swipe up from a bottom corner instead.
  2. Say your reminder. For example: “Remind me to call Mom at noon tomorrow.”
  3. Check it. Gemini saves reminders in Google Tasks (on many Samsung phones, in Samsung Reminder). You’ll get a notification when it’s time.
    • The first time, Gemini may ask to connect to your Google apps. Follow the steps on screen if you want reminders to work.

New to Gemini? See My Android Phone Has Gemini Instead of Google Assistant. What Changed?

With Alexa (Echo speaker)

  1. Say “Alexa,” then your reminder. For example: “Alexa, remind me to take out the trash at 7 p.m.”
  2. Alexa will say the reminder out loud on that same speaker when it’s time.
  3. To see or change reminders, open the Alexa app on your phone, tap More, then Reminders.

If something goes wrong

  • The assistant didn’t hear me. Speak a little slower, and say the time clearly, like “three p.m.”
  • I didn’t get the reminder. Check that the phone isn’t on silent or Do Not Disturb, and that notifications are allowed for Reminders (iPhone) or Google Tasks and the Google app (Android).
  • “Hey Siri” or “Hey Google” doesn’t work. It may be turned off. On iPhone, look in Settings under Siri (or Apple Intelligence & Siri). On Android, open the Gemini app’s settings and look for Hey Google & Voice Match.
  • I want to delete a reminder. iPhone: in the Reminders app, swipe left on it and tap Delete. Alexa: say “Alexa, delete my reminder.”

Official sources

  • Apple: Use Reminders on your iPhone (including Siri)
  • Google: Capture your tasks & reminders with Gemini Apps
  • Google: The Assistant experience on mobile is upgrading to Gemini
  • Amazon: Set Reminders with Alexa
