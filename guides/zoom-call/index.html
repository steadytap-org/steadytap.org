How to Join a Zoom Call for Family or Club

What this helps with: Joining a Zoom video call from a link someone sent you, on a phone, tablet, or computer.

You don’t need a Zoom account to join someone else’s call. You only need the link they sent, or the meeting ID and passcode.

Before the call (do this once)

  1. Get the Zoom app. On a phone or tablet, open the App Store (iPhone or iPad) or Play Store (Android) and search for Zoom Workplace. Tap Get or Install.
    • What you’ll tap: The app’s icon is a white video camera on a blue square.
    • On a computer, you can skip the app and join from your web browser instead.
  2. Find the invitation. Look for an email, text, or calendar invite that says Join Zoom Meeting with a link under it. Write the meeting ID and passcode on paper if they’re listed.

Join the call

  1. Tap the link in the invitation a few minutes before the start time.
  2. If asked, tap Open to open it in the Zoom app. On a computer, you can also click Join from your browser.
  3. If asked for your name, type the name you’d like others to see, like “Grandma Rose.”
  4. Allow the camera and microphone if your device asks. Tap Allow or OK. Zoom needs these so family can see and hear you.
  5. Check your video and sound, then tap Join.
  6. If you see “Waiting for the host,” that’s normal. Stay on the screen. The host will let you in.
  7. Turn on your sound: If you can’t hear anyone, tap Join Audio, then Wi-Fi or Cellular Data (or Call using Internet Audio).
    • What you’ll tap: The buttons along the bottom of the screen. Tap the screen once if they’re hidden.
  8. Mute and unmute: Tap the microphone button at the bottom left. A red line means others can’t hear you.
  9. When you’re done, tap Leave (top right on a phone, bottom right on a computer), then Leave meeting.

No smartphone? Join by phone call

If the invitation lists a phone number, call it from any phone. When asked, type the meeting ID and press #, then the passcode and #. You’ll hear the call, but there’s no video.

If something goes wrong

  • “Invalid meeting ID.” Check the numbers carefully, or tap the link instead of typing.
  • No one can hear me. Make sure the microphone button doesn’t have a red line. Tap Join Audio if you see it.
  • I can’t see the others. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger picture. Check your Wi-Fi.
  • The picture freezes. Move closer to your Wi-Fi router, or turn your video off for a moment.
  • Someone you don’t know sends a Zoom link asking you to “verify your account” or pay. Don’t tap it. A real Zoom invitation only asks you to join a call.
